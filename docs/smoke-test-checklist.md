# Riverbend Workshop Smoke Test Checklist

Use this checklist after each milestone update. The player should be able to complete it without typing notes.

1. Load the app and confirm the playable field is visible without page scrolling.
2. Select each tool and confirm the selected state differs from the recommended state.
3. In Survey, choose an excavation area and confirm guidance updates.
4. In Excavation, drag the brush across nearby find contexts and confirm soil masks visibly clear.
5. Switch to the fine pick and confirm compact contexts clear faster while risky use can reduce certainty.
6. Stabilize a fragile find and confirm preservation feedback appears before lifting.
7. Lift an exposed find and confirm it moves to storage with context recorded.
8. Use Seed tray to recover the representative set.
9. Open Examination and clean/rotate the selected find until authored close-up detail becomes readable.
10. Toggle sound and confirm brushing, lifting, joining, and reconstruction cues respect the setting.
11. Open Join, drag and rotate a recovered pair toward the ghost seam, then confirm a snap.
12. Group cooking, textile, closure, and disturbance evidence.
13. Open Reconstruction and place supported zones only after matching grouped evidence and required joins are present.
14. Confirm placed reconstruction zones render staged room states: hearth, bench, threshold, pit, and collapse.
15. Open Interpretation, choose each explanation, and confirm confidence and contradictions update.
16. Start each Fieldwork history and confirm the selected seed label, objective, and starter tray change while using the same evidence logic.
17. Save, reload, and confirm the restored state is not overwritten by the default opening state.
18. Check desktop, tablet, and mobile widths for no required browser-page scrolling during active play.
19. On mobile, open the Context drawer and confirm selected find, storage, confidence, and quick navigation are reachable.
